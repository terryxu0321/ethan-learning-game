## A. NSW Department of Education: Best Start Kindergarten

- **[S1]** *Best Start Kindergarten Assessment* (teacher/curriculum page). NSW Department of Education. Undated, current in 2026. https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/assessment-resources/best-start-kindergarten (older path: …/literacy-and-numeracy/best-start). Official web page. © State of NSW (Department of Education); the department's site is generally CC BY 4.0 unless marked otherwise (需核实 for this page). Note: a one-to-one, teacher-led interview held in the first 5 weeks of Kindergarten in all NSW public schools. It has literacy and numeracy components of about 20 minutes each. It is "mapped to the National Literacy and Numeracy Learning Progressions" and aligned to the NSW English and Mathematics syllabuses. Results are recorded in PLAN2 against progression indicators.
- **[S2]** *Best Start Kindergarten Assessment* (parent page). NSW Department of Education. https://education.nsw.gov.au/schooling/parents-and-carers/going-to-school/getting-ready-for-school/starting-primary-school/learning-at-primary-school/best-start-kindergarten-assessment. Official parent information. Same licence note as S1. Note: children do not need to prepare or study, and it is not a test; parents can download a report.
- **[S3]** *NSW Department of Education Best Start Kindergarten Assessment* (English factsheet, part of the translated set). https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/going-to-a-public-school/media/documents/translated-documents-/best-start-kindergarten-assessment/english-best-start-kindergarten-assessment-factsheet.pdf. Official factsheet (PDF). Same licence note as S1. Note: literacy tasks cover writing one's own name, recognising sounds, letters and familiar words, and recalling details of a short book read aloud. Numeracy tasks cover counting, counting a number of objects, adding or subtracting small numbers and recognising repeating patterns. The task list was seen through search snippets; open the PDF to confirm.
- **[S4]** NSW public-school Kindergarten transition pages that describe Best Start, e.g. Blacktown South PS https://sites.google.com/education.nsw.gov.au/bsps-online-learning/kindergarten-orientation/best-start-assessment ; BHPS Kindergarten 2027 https://sites.google.com/education.nsw.gov.au/kindergarten2027atbhps/best-start. School web pages (secondary). © the schools. Note: one school page gives a sample rhyme item: "Listen to these words: map, tap. Can you think of another word that rhymes?" Which page carries it is 需核实.
- **[S5]** *Overview of statewide assessments*. NSW Department of Education. https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/assessment/available-assessments/overview-state-wide-assessments. Official page. Note: places Best Start Kindergarten, the Year 1 Phonics Screening Check and other assessments on a single timeline.
- **[S6]** Catholic Schools NSW, *Best Start Kindergarten Assessment (BSKA)*. https://csnsw.catholic.edu.au/learning-and-wellbeing/student-assessment/best-start-kindergarten-assessment-bska. Sector page. © CSNSW. Note: non-government schools can opt in, and their items are auto-mapped in PLAN2 to the progressions and the K–2 syllabuses. This matters because the family will move to an independent school later.

## B. National Literacy and Numeracy Learning Progressions (V3)

- **[S7]** *National Literacy Learning Progression*, Version 3. ACARA (NSW DoE-hosted PDF). 2020 (V3). https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/en/home/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/resources-for-schools/lnlp/national-literacy-learning-progression-v3-for-publication.pdf ; ACARA web version: https://v8.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/national-literacy-and-numeracy-learning-progressions/national-literacy-learning-progression/ . Official framework. ACARA material is published under CC BY 4.0 per ACARA's terms (需核实 for the progression PDF). Note: sub-elements seen in snippets: Listening (LiS), Interacting (InT), Speaking (SpK), Understanding texts (UnT), Phonological awareness (PhA), Phonic knowledge and word recognition (PKW), Creating texts (CrT). Indicators within a level are "non-hierarchical". Snippets gave the PhA1, PhA2, PKW1, PKW2, PKW3, LiS1, SpK1 and CrT1 indicators quoted in findings.
- **[S8]** *National Numeracy Learning Progression*, Version 3. ACARA (NSW DoE-hosted PDF). https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/en/home/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/resources-for-schools/lnlp/national-numeracy-learning-progression-v3-for-publication.pdf ; ACARA web: https://v8.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/national-literacy-and-numeracy-learning-progressions/national-numeracy-learning-progression/ . Official framework. Same licence note as S7. Note: the counting sub-element code is **CPr** (Counting processes), **not "CoP"**. Quantifying numbers is **QuN**. Snippets gave the CPr1 and CPr2 indicators and the perceptual-counting and subitising indicators. AdS (Additive strategies) and NPA (Number patterns and algebraic thinking) appear in secondary listings. "UuM" was not confirmed.
- **[S9]** *About the Literacy and Numeracy Learning Progressions*. NSW Department of Education. https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/resources-for-schools/learning-progressions . Official page.
- **[S10]** *Early Stage 1 Speaking and listening*. NSW Department of Education (policies site). https://policies.education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/english/speaking-and-listening/early-stage-1-speaking-and-listening . Official teaching resource, **written for the 2012 syllabus** (outcome ENe-1A). Note: maps ES1 to LiS2–LiS4, InT1–InT3, SpK2–SpK3 and PhA1. A snippet also said a companion ES1 page maps UnT2–UnT4 and CrT1–CrT3; which page that is remains 需核实.
- **[S11]** NNLP "Quantifying numbers" I-can statements. Calculate (ACT Education Directorate). 2018. https://calculate.org.au/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2018/11/nnlp-qun-i-can-statements.pdf . Adaptation of the progression (secondary, based on V2). © ACT Government. Note: the QuN1 adaptation includes subitising a small quantity of fewer than 4, comparing two collections and matching numerals.
- **[S12]** Queensland QCAA, *Sequence of numeracy progressions* / *Sequence of literacy progressions*. https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/aciqv9/general-resources/ac9_gc_progressions_numeracy.pdf ; https://www.qcaa.qld.edu.au/downloads/aciqv9/general-resources/ac9_gc_progressions_literacy.pdf . Reformatted copy from a state authority (V3/AC9-aligned). © QCAA / ACARA. Note: includes the perceptual-counting indicator about keeping track of counted and uncounted items, e.g. moving each block aside as it is counted.

## D. Year 1 Phonics Screening Check (next milestone)

- **[S21]** *Year 1 Phonics Screening Check*. NSW Department of Education. https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/assessment-resources/phonics-screening-check ; parent factsheet https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/going-to-a-public-school/media/documents/translated-documents-/phonics-screening-check/english-phonics-screening.pdf . Official. Same licence note as S1. Note: 40 words (20 real, 20 pseudo). Held in Term 3, Weeks 4–6 of Year 1. Pseudo-words test blending rather than memory.
- **[S22]** *Year 1 Phonics Screening Check 2025 update* (CESE), plus the 2022, 2023 and 2024 updates. NSW DoE CESE. https://education.nsw.gov.au/about-us/education-data-and-research/cese/publications/research-reports/year-1-phonics-screening-check-2025 ; PDF https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/about-us/educational-data/cese/2025_phonics_screening_check.pdf . Official statistical report. Note: expected level is ≥28/40. 64% met it in 2025 (60% in 2024, 59% in 2023, 55% in 2022); 2025 average was 28.5. A third-party blog's "32" is a UK figure or an error; do not use it.
- **[S23]** *Phonics diagnostic assessment*. NSW DoE. https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/assessment-resources/phonics-diagnostic-assessment . Official. Note: an optional K–2 phonics assessment; useful as a reference for in-app checks (content 需核实).

## E. AEDC and EYLF (the "minimum / on track" anchor)

- **[S24]** *Definition of AEDC terms*. Australian Government (AEDC). https://www.aedc.gov.au/docs/default-source/default-document-library/aedc_definition-of-terms_da6.pdf . Official. © Commonwealth; reuse terms 需核实. Note: cut-offs are fixed from the 2009 census. Below the 10th percentile is "developmentally vulnerable", the 10th–25th percentile is "at risk", and above the 25th percentile is "on track".
- **[S25]** *AEDC National Report 2024*. Australian Government. 2025. https://www.aedc.gov.au/docs/default-source/default-document-library/aedc_national-report-2024_da7-409a37b9d-95ce-43e7-af00-51aa604cf0b3.pdf ; news: https://www.aedc.gov.au/news-and-media/details/the-2024-aedc-results-have-been-released . Official. Note: national vulnerability is 7.7% on Language & cognitive skills (school-based), up 0.4 points from 2021, and 8.9% on Communication skills & general knowledge. 81.7% are on track on Language & cognitive (snippet).
- **[S26]** *AEDC NSW Report 2024*. NSW DoE. https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/en/home/schooling/curriculum/early-learning/aedc-2024-nsw-data-report.pdf . Official. Note (snippet; confirm against the PDF): NSW vulnerability is 7.0% on Language & cognitive (+0.8 points) and 8.7% on Communication (+0.3). On track is 83.1% on Language & cognitive, the highest of all domains. Domain definitions: Language & cognitive = "interest and skills in literacy and numeracy, and memory".
- **[S27]** *Domain Guide: Language and Cognitive Skills (school-based)*. AEDC (NSW-hosted copy) https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/en/home/student-wellbeing/whole-school-approach/aedc/image/DOMAIN_GUIDE_Language_and_cognitive_skills.pdf ; SA copy https://www.education.sa.gov.au/docs/system-performance/aedc/aedc-sa-domain-guide-language.pdf ; *About the AEDC domains* https://www.aedc.gov.au/docs/default-source/default-document-library/about-the-aedc-domains.pdf . Official. Note: four sub-domains are basic literacy, advanced literacy, basic numeracy, and interest in literacy/numeracy & memory. Item wording was not seen in snippets (需核实).
- **[S28]** *EDI Interpretation Toolkit*. Offord Centre for Child Studies (McMaster). https://edi-offordcentre.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/2019/03/EDI-interpretation-toolkit.pdf . The Canadian parent instrument the Australian AvEDI is adapted from (secondary for Australia). © Offord Centre. Note: items include Qb15 "Is able to read simple words", Qb29 "Is able to count to 20" and Qb30 "Is able to recognize [numbers] 1–10". Australian AvEDI item wording may differ (需核实).
- **[S29]** *Research Spotlight: AEDC 2024*. Mitchell Institute, Victoria University. 2025. https://www.vu.edu.au/mitchell-institute/education/schooling/research-spotlight-australian-early-development-census-2024 . Research summary (secondary). Note: on track on Language & cognitive means "interested in books, reading, writing and math".
- **[S30]** *Which skills are important for future literacy and numeracy learning?* (AEDC–NAPLAN linkage). Australian Government DSS/ERIC. https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED673988.pdf . Research report. Note: Language & cognitive is the AEDC domain most strongly associated with Year 3 NAPLAN, and basic literacy and basic numeracy predict Year 3 reading.
- **[S31]** *Belonging, Being and Becoming: The Early Years Learning Framework for Australia* V2.0. Australian Government Department of Education / ACECQA. 2022. https://www.acecqa.gov.au/node/1088181 (exact PDF URL 需核实). National framework. CC BY 4.0 for text with photos excluded (per research/03 S35; 需核实). Note: Outcome 5 "Children are effective communicators" includes 5.4 "begin to understand how symbols and pattern systems work". Outcome 4 "confident and involved learners" covers dispositions, problem-solving and transfer. It sets no numeric literacy or numeracy benchmarks.
