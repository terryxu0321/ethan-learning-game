## A. 学习科学 Learning science

**[S1]** Hirsh-Pasek, K., Zosh, J. M., Golinkoff, R. M., Gray, J. H., Robb, M. B., & Kaufman, J. — *Putting Education in "Educational" Apps: Lessons From the Science of Learning*. Psychological Science in the Public Interest 16(1), 3–34. 2015. DOI https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100615569721 ; APS 页面 https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/educational-apps.html
- Type: peer-reviewed review. License: "© The Author(s) 2015"，Sage reprints & permissions —— **未发现 CC 许可，按版权所有处理，未保存全文**。
- Note: 提出 "Four Pillars"：active（主动思考而非仅点击）、engaged（不被花哨功能分心）、meaningful（与生活经验相关）、socially interactive（与他人互动），并以清晰学习目标为前提。

**[S2]** Meyer, M., Zosh, J. M., McLaren, C., Robb, M., McCafferty, H., Golinkoff, R. M., Hirsh-Pasek, K., & Radesky, J. — *How educational are "educational" apps for young children? **[S3]** Piasta, S. B., & Wagner, R. **[S4]** Vlach, H. A., Sandhofer, C. M., & Kornell, N. (2008) *The spacing effect in children's memory and category induction*, Cognition；Vlach & Sandhofer (2012) *Distributing learning over time: The spacing effect in children's acquisition and generalization of science concepts*, Child Development. - Type: peer-reviewed. License: 未核实 —— 未保存。

- Type: peer-reviewed meta-analysis. License: APA 版权 —— 未保存。
- Note: 128 项实验；与参与/完成/表现挂钩的"预期性有形奖励"降低自由选择情境下的内在动机（摘要中 d 约 −0.28 至 −0.40）；**正面的信息性反馈（verbal positive feedback）提升内在动机**。注意 Cameron & Pierce 一派对此有争议。

**[S6]** Gelman, R., & Gallistel, C. R. (1978) *The Child's Understanding of Number*. Harvard University Press. （五条计数原则；经 Stanford PreK Math、NZ Maths 等教学资源转述）

- Note: one-to-one、stable order、cardinality、abstraction、order irrelevance；5–6 岁儿童仍可能未完全掌握全部原则。

**[S8]** U.S. Dept. of Education, IES/WWC — *Teaching Math to Young Children* (NCEE 2014-4005). 2013. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/18
